Stuffed Sweet Potato

Prep: 6 min | Cook: 3 min

Ingredients

  • 1 Sweet Potato
  • 1/3 cup Canned Beans
  • cheese, pre-grated
  • sweet paprika

Instructions

  1. Poke a generous amount of holes in sweet potato with a fork (the more holes, the faster and more even it cooks), and heat in microwave on high for 6 minutes
  2. Once finished, slice potato in half. Stuff with cheese and beans
  3. Finish with a sprinkling of cheese on top, followed by paprika
  4. Enjoy!
