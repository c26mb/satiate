Chickpea Medley Salad

Prep: 4 min | Cook: 2 min

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup chickpeas
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 cup kale, pre-chopped
  • 1/4 cup tomato, diced
  • 1/4 cup avocado, diced
  • 1/4 cup cucumber, diced
  • pinch of salt
  • pinch of pepper
  • 1-2 tbsp olive oil

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, add chickpeas and water. Put in microwave and heat on high for 2 minutes to cook the chickpeas.
  2. In another bowl, add in kale, tomatoes, avocado, and cucumber
  3. Strain chickpeas making sure to rinse well and add them into the salad. Sprinkle on salt and pepper. Then, drizzle on olive oil. Enjoy!
