Mango Rice Pudding

Prep: 8 min | Cook: 1 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water
  • 1/2 cup rice
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1/3 cup milk

Instructions

  1. Place rice and water in a microwave rice cooker, heat for 6 minutes until almost cooked
  2. Add milk and sugar to rice, mix, and place into microwave again to heat for 3 minutes
  3. Place cooked rice pudding into a bowl, finish with mango on top, and enjoy!
