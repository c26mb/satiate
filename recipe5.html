Lemon Blueberry Yogurt Parfait

Prep: 0 min | Cook: 2 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup greek yogurt
  • 1 cup granola
  • 2-3 tbsp lemon curd
  • 1 handful blueberries

Instructions

  1. Add yogurt to the base of a cup, layering granola, then lemon curd. Repeat this step twice
  2. Add the blueberries to the very top, and add a little more crushed granola on top
  3. Enjoy!
