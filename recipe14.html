Chocolate Mug Cake

Prep: 2 min | Cook: 2 min

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp flour
  • 2 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1/4 tsp baking powder
  • dash salt
  • 3 tbsp milk
  • 1 tbsp canola oil
  • 1/8 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tsp chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Add flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking powder, and salt into a microwave safe 16oz mug and stir together until combined
  2. Add milk, canola oil, vanilla extract, and stir until it makes a smooth batter. Stir in chocolate chips afterwards
  3. Cook in microwave for 70-90 seconds until cake has just set, let it rest for 1 minute, sprinkle on some chocolate chips as garnishment and enjoy!
