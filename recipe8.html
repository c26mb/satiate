Spaghetti (Squash) Delight

Prep: 10 min | Cook: 2 min

Ingredients

  • 1/2 spaghetti squash
  • 1/3 cup water
  • 1 tomato
  • cottage cheese
  • 1/4 tsp basil
  • 1/4 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Instructions

  1. Add water to a microwave-safe bowl with a flat bottom. Place half of spaghetti squash with skin facing up into the bowl, and microwave for 10 min
  2. Dice tomato while waiting for spaghetti squash
  3. Once finished heating and skin is soft, take a fork and carve out middle of spaghetti squash until it looks spaghetti-like
  4. Add diced tomato, cottage cheese, and seasoning to taste. Enjoy!
