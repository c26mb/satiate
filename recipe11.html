French Toast Bites

Prep: 3 min | Cook: 2 min

Ingredients

  • 2 slices brioche bread
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1/4 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 handful blueberries/li>

Instructions

  1. Slice brioche bread into 1/2 inch pieces and set aside
  2. Mix together egg, maple syrup, vanilla, and milk until batter is smooth and fully combined
  3. Add brioche bread into batter, making sure every peice is coated evenly. Heat in microwave for 1:30-2 minutes or until batter is fully cooked
  4. Sprinkle cinnamon and blueberries on top, and enjoy!
