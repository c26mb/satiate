Watermelon Feta Salad

Prep: 1 min | Cook: 2 min

Ingredients

  • 1/3 cup watermelon
  • 1/4 cup feta cheese
  • 1 cup kale
  • 1-2 tbsp balsamic vinaigrette
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, add together kale, watermelon, and feta
  2. Drizzle olive oil and balsamic vinaigrette on top of salad
  3. Enjoy!
