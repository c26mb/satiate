Cinnamon Blueberry Banana Oats

Prep: 2 min | Cook: 1 min

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup quick oats
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 banana, sliced
  • 1 handful blueberries
  • 1-2 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 cinnamon stick (optional)

Instructions

  1. Add oats and water into a bowl and microwave for around 1 minute until ready. Make sure to use a bowl that is large enough to let the oats rise without overflowing
  2. Add chopped banana and blueberries to the oatmeal
  3. Drizzle maple syrup and sprinkle ground cinnamon on top
  4. Place the cinnamon stick in if desired (very unnecessary) and enjoy!
