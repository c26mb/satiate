Vanilla Mug Cake

Prep: 2 min | Cook: 2 min

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup flour
  • 2 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1/4 tsp baking powder
  • dash salt
  • 2 tbsp melted butter
  • 3 tbsp milk
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tsp sprinkles

Instructions

  1. Add flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt into a microwave safe 16oz mug and stir together until combined
  2. Add milk, melted butter, and vanilla extract, then stir until it makes a smooth batter. Stir in sprinkles afterwards
  3. Cook in microwave for 70-90 seconds until cake has just set, let it rest for 1 minute, sprinkle on some sprinkles as garnishment and enjoy!
