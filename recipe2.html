Tomato Cottage Toast

Prep: 2 min | Cook: 1 min

Ingredients

  • 1 Slice Sourdough Bread
  • Cottage Cheese
  • 1 Tomato
  • 1 Tbsp Basil Pesto
  • Olive Oil
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat up Sourdough Bread (or don't, it's delicious either way!), you can use a microwave to do this if a toaster is not accessible
  2. Grab some Cottage Cheese and spread it across your heated bread, add as much as you like
  3. Chop the tomato into small pieces and add as many or as little as you desire
  4. Drizzle on some olive oil and finish with a sprinkle of salt and pepper, enjoy!
