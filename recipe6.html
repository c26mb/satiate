Accordion Cucumber Bowl

Prep: 8 min | Cook: 2 min

Ingredients

  • 2 cucumbers
  • 1/2 cup rice
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp chili oil
  • 1 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds

Instructions

  1. Add rice and water into a rice cooker, heat in microwave for 8 minutes
  2. While rice is cooking, chop the ends off the cucumbers. Then, one at a time, place a cucumber between two chopsticks and create even slices vertically on one side. Flip over, and create even vertical slices at a diagonal angle. Do this for both cucumbers
  3. Combine soy sauce, garlic, rice vinegar, chili oil, sugar, sesame oil, and sesame seeds in a bowl
  4. Once rice has finished cooking, remove and place in a separate bowl, adding cucumbers to that bowl and pouring soy sauce mixture over top
  5. Place egg in a bowl with a flat bottom and cook in microwave for 20 second intervals until to your liking
  6. Add egg on top of rice, and enjoy!
