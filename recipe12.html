Cheese Veggie Tortilla

Prep: __ min | Cook: __ min

Ingredients

  • 1 tortilla bread
  • 1/4 cup mexican cheddar jack
  • 1 jalapeño
  • 1 tomato
  • 2 lettuce leaves
  • 1/2 tsp cilantro
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Sprinkle on first layer of cheddar jack and warm tortilla in a microwave for 30 seconds
  2. Add on jalapeño, tomato, and lettuce leaves
  3. Sprinkle on another garnishing layer of cheddar jack, cilantro, black pepper, salt, and enjoy!
