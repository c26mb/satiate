Vegetable Peanut Kelp Noodles

Prep: 2 min | Cook: 3 min

Ingredients

  • 1 package kelp noodles
  • 1/4 cup lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp baking soda
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tbsp lime juice
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 1 clove garlic, grated
  • 1/4 cup creamy peanut butter
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 2-3 tbsp water
  • 1 carrot, sliced (optional)
  • 1 cucumber, sliced (optional)
  • 1 scallion, diced (optional)

Instructions

  1. Mix together lemon juice, baking soda, and cup of water into a bowl. Add in kelp noodles and swish around mixture until noodles have separated and softened. Once noodles have reached the desired texture, strain them
  2. In another bowl, add lime juice, soy sauce, rice vinegar, honey ginger, and garlic. Whisk until well combined.
  3. Add in the peanut butter, olive oil, and sesame oil, and whisk until very well combined.
  4. Pour in 2-3tbsp of water to loosen sauce to desired thickness. Then, add in noodles and mix until coated well.
  5. If desired, add sliced carrot, cucumber, and scallion to the noodles, and enjoy!
